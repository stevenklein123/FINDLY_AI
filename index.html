What is Findly AI?

A web-based lost and found platform. Findly AI compares photos of lost and found items, suggests possible matches, and notifies both people before the Lost and Found Office checks who owns the item.

What you can do

  • Report lost and found items online
  • Upload photos and descriptions
  • Get AI image matching
  • Get notified of potential matches via email

How Findly AI works

Here’s what happens after you report an item.

1

Report

Report a lost or found item online. Upload a photo and description.

2

AI Compares

Findly AI compares uploaded images using image recognition and visual similarity analysis.

3

Get Notified

If there is a potential match, both users are notified by email.

4

Verify Ownership

The Lost and Found Office performs ownership verification.

Lost & Found Guidelines

A few simple tips to help Findly AI find your item.

For best result

  • Upload clear images.
  • Add a description with your photo.
  • Check your email for match notifications.
  • Visit the Lost and Found Office for ownership verification.

Limitation

  • AI provides potential matches only.
  • Final ownership requires manual verification.
  • Items with few visual features may reduce accuracy.
  • Internet access is required for uploads and notification.
